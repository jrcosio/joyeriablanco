## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the nocturnal exclusivity and surgical precision of an elite private jewelry atelier. Tailored for managing high-value bespoke commissions, prestigious client dossiers, and luxury invoicing, the aesthetic pairs the uncompromising darkness of deep volcanic charcoal with the warm, luminescent brilliance of brushed antique gold.

The visual direction merges high-fashion editorial rigor with functional software utility:
- **Architectural Brutalism meets Luxury Minimal:** Razor-sharp 90-degree corners, immaculate planar surfaces, and hairline structural dividers convey mechanical craftsmanship and metallurgical accuracy.
- **Atmospheric Contrast:** Immersive velvet dark surfaces evoke a private vault viewing room, ensuring client assets, gemstone certifications, and financial figures appear vivid and luminous.
- **Tone & Mood:** Discreet, uncompromising, disciplined, and bespoke. Every interaction reinforces trust, permanence, and high aesthetic refinement.

## Colors

> **Single source of truth:** the color tokens in the frontmatter are normative. This prose
> refers to them by name (hex shown only as a reminder). Translucent values are expressed as a
> token plus an opacity (e.g. `primary-container` @ 18%). Any color not expressible this way
> requires amending the frontmatter first.

The color palette is anchored in charcoal depth and metallic warmth, tuned specifically to balance administrative legibility with boutique luxury:

- **Primary (`primary` `#efbf6b` - Luminous Gold):** Focal actions (primary buttons), active states, key financial totals, and prestige identifiers. Text on it uses `on-primary`.
- **Antique Gold (`primary-container` `#b48a3c`):** Brushed-gold accents: hairline borders, focus rings, currency symbols, and brass-accented summary boxes.
- **Radiant Gold (`tertiary` `#e9c349`):** Hover states of primary actions, selected borders, and subtle glow states.
- **Silver (`secondary` `#c3c6d1`):** Neutral secondary icons and metadata where gold would be excessive.
- **Surfaces:** elevated containment (cards, trays, inputs) uses the `surface-container-*` scale, from `surface-container-lowest` `#0b0e14` to `surface-container-highest` `#32353b`.
- **Neutral Dark Canvas (`background` / `surface` `#101319`):** The foundational substrate, evoking onyx and obsidian vaults.
- **Text:** `on-surface` `#e1e2eb` for primary text, `on-surface-variant` `#d2c5b3` for muted text.
- **Semantic Feedback:**
  - *Emerald (`success` `#10b981`):* Confirmed receipts, settled balances, verified appraisal status, active records.
  - *Amber (`warning` `#f59e0b`):* Open estimates, work-in-progress smithing, pending authorizations.
  - *Crimson (`danger` `#ef4444`):* Overdue invoices, critical valuation alerts, destructive actions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances dramatic editorial pedigree with technical legibility:

- **Headlines (Bodoni Moda):** An extreme-contrast, high-fashion Didone serif that delivers prestige to invoice headers, catalog collections, valuation summaries, and total sums. It brings the presence of a couture luxury house to enterprise workflows.
- **Operational Copy & Data (Manrope):** A modernist, low-flair geometric sans-serif that remains razor-sharp across tabular data, pricing grids, inventory codes, and form inputs.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Form labels, status indicators, and currency tags utilize `Manrope` in uppercase with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.16em`) to echo fine watchmaking serials and high-jewelry engravings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column grid prioritizing expansive breathing room and balanced negative space:

- **Horizontal Rhythm:** Fluid desktop margins set to `2.5rem` provide framing akin to a passe-partout mat board, ensuring ledger grids and client dossiers do not press against viewport edges.
- **Column Logic:** 12 columns with a `1.5rem` gutter on desktop allow modular layouts (e.g., 8-column itemization breakdown paired with a 4-column client and payment summary).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - *Mobile (< 768px):* Collapses to a 4-column grid with `1rem` margins and gutters; side-by-side action bars convert into stacked full-width controls.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1024px):* 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters.
  - *Desktop (> 1024px):* 12 columns, max content boundary capped at `1440px` for optimal viewing distance and clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through surgical planar stacking and hairline perimeter illumination, deliberately eliminating soft, puffy shadows in favor of crisp lapidary precision:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `background` (`#101319`) serving as the uninterrupted foundation.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Cards):** `surface-container` (`#1d2026`) enclosed by a 1px hairline border of `primary-container` @ 18%.
- **Level 2 (Active Modals & Contextual Trays):** `surface-container-high` (`#272a30`) outlined in `tertiary` @ 35%, accompanied by a tight, directional drop shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Inspection Overlays & Tooltips):** `surface-container-lowest` (`#0b0e14`) with an exact 1px solid `primary-container` border and zero ambient blur, creating a crisp jewel-loupe visual focus.

## Shapes

The geometric framework adheres to absolute angularity:

- **Zero Curvature (`roundedness: 0`):** Every container, input, modal, badge, and button maintains strictly sharp 90-degree corners.
- **Visual Rationale:** Emulates precision emerald-cut gemstones, architectural vault doors, and hand-cut parchment receipts. Sharp edges accentuate high-contrast typography and eliminate the casual softness of standard consumer interfaces.
- **Dividers & Strokes:** Universal border thickness is locked to 1px hairline rules, reinforcing mechanical exactness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `primary` background with `on-primary` uppercase `Manrope` typography (`label-lg`, bold, letter-spaced). Flat, sharp 90-degree rectangle. On hover: solid `tertiary` with subtle golden aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px` `tertiary` @ 30%).
- **Secondary (Vault Outline):** Transparent background with 1px border in `primary-container` and `on-surface` text. On hover: interior fill shifts to `primary-container` @ 8%.
- **Ghost / Destructive:** `on-surface-variant` text with instantaneous color transition to `danger` on interaction, accompanied by a 1px `danger` bottom hairline.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Structure:** Sharp rectangular containers filled with `surface-container-low` (`#191c22`) and framed by a 1px stroke of `on-surface` @ 12%.
- **State Changes:** On focus, the border shifts to crisp `primary-container` with zero outer blur. Floating labels transition to `label-sm` in `primary`.
- **Monetary Inputs:** Currency symbol (`€` / `$`) locked in fixed-width `primary-container`, with numeric inputs rendered in tabular figures for ledger alignment.

### Data Tables & Invoicing Grids
- **Container & Rows:** `surface-container-low` background.
- **Header Row:** `surface-container` background, uppercase tracking (`label-md`), separated by a 1px horizontal rule in `primary-container` @ 25%.
- **Data Rows:** Hover highlight `surface-container-high` with zero vertical borders, separated strictly by horizontal hairlines. Ample padding (`1rem` vertical, `1.5rem` horizontal) guarantees relaxed scannability during complex stone itemization.
- **Totals Section:** Emphasized display using `Bodoni Moda` for the grand total figure, framed within a dedicated `primary-container`-accented summary box.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Form:** Strict 0px radius micro-rectangles, framed by a 1px hairline border.
- **Paid / Approved / Active:** Background `success` @ 8%, border `success` @ 40%, text `success`.
- **Pending / In Atelier:** Background `warning` @ 8%, border `warning` @ 40%, text `warning`.
- **Overdue / Expired / Inactive:** Background `danger` @ 8%, border `danger` @ 40%, text `danger`.
- **Neutral (Voided / Superseded):** Background `on-surface-variant` @ 8%, border
  `on-surface-variant` @ 40%, text `on-surface-variant`. A discreet mark for documents that are
  kept but no longer in force: voided or corrected invoices, and voided or superseded estimates.
- **Estimates:** draft and «in invoicing» use the Pending tone, expired the Expired tone,
  converted into an invoice the Approved tone, and voided or superseded the Neutral tone.

### Cards & Dossiers
- Flat `surface-container` background bounded by a 1px rule. Sections within cards (e.g., client provenance, gemological certificate links) are segmented by hairline internal dividers with `space-md` internal margins.

## Paper (print and PDF)

Printed documents (invoices, estimates and their listings) are rendered as PDF on white
paper. The application itself keeps its single dark theme; this section applies only to paper.

- **Colors:** `paper` is the page; `paper-ink` (derived from `inverse-on-surface`) is the main
  text and `paper-ink-muted` (derived from `secondary-container`) the labels and secondary text.
  `paper-accent` (derived from `inverse-primary`) is the legible antique gold on white for the
  grand total and section accents. `paper-rule` (derived from `primary-container`) is the colour
  of every hairline and of the totals frame. `paper-alert` (derived from `error-container`) is
  reserved for status marks such as «ANULADA», «RECTIFICADA» or «DUPLICADO» on invoices, and
  «ANULADO», «SUSTITUIDO» or «CONVERTIDO» on estimates. `paper-qr` is pure black, for maximum
  contrast of the tax QR code on white; estimates never carry a QR code.
- **Typography:** sizes are in points. `print-title` (Bodoni Moda) for the document title and
  `print-total` (Bodoni Moda) for the grand total; everything else in Manrope: `print-body` for
  data, `print-body-strong` for emphasised data, `print-heading` and `print-label` in uppercase
  with letter-spacing for headings and labels, `print-table` for tabular rows and `print-small`
  for footers and notes. Figures are always tabular.
- **Shapes and depth:** no shadows and no coloured backgrounds except the totals frame; corners at
  0; every rule is a 1px hairline (`0.75pt`) in `paper-rule`.
- **Layout:** A4, `print-margin` page margins and `print-gutter` between columns. Data tables
  follow «Data Tables & Invoicing Grids»: uppercase header labels, horizontal hairlines only, and
  the grand total in Bodoni Moda inside a `paper-rule`-framed summary box.
- **Status marks:** small uppercase `print-label` text in `paper-alert` inside a 1px `paper-alert`
  box with 0 radius. Marks never overlap other content (no watermarks).
- **Non-fiscal notice (estimates):** directly under the document title, the sentence «Documento
  sin validez fiscal. No es una factura.» in `print-body-strong` and `paper-ink`, inside a 1px
  `paper-rule` box with 0 radius and `print-gutter` / 2 padding. It is information, not a status
  mark, so it never uses `paper-alert`, and it is never a watermark.
